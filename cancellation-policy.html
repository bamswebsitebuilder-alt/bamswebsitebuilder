Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains how to request cancellation and how payments, completed work, and handover are reviewed. Read your project’s specific terms before paying.

How to cancel

Email bamswebsitebuilder@gmail.com with your name, project or service, and the date you want to cancel. You may also call 727-417-8762; please follow up by email so there is a written record. We will confirm receipt and explain any remaining balance, refund, and handover steps.

Custom projects and deposits

If you cancel a custom website project, we will review the work completed, payments received, and any third-party expenses you approved. Your accepted written quote or project agreement determines what is payable and refundable. A deposit is applied toward the project; it is not automatically forfeited just because you cancel. Any nonrefundable amount or cancellation charge must have been disclosed and agreed to before payment.

Monthly website payment plans

Our advertised website plans consist of 12 monthly payments toward a website project. They are not advertised as month-to-month maintenance services. Canceling automatic payments does not by itself cancel the project agreement. Any remaining amount due on early cancellation depends on the written terms you accepted; this page does not create an automatic requirement to pay every remaining installment. Contact us before checkout if you need those terms clarified.

PayPal automatic payments

You can manage or cancel automatic payments in your PayPal account. See PayPal’s instructions. Please also email BAM’s to cancel the project or service and arrange any balance or handover. Stopping future automatic payments does not automatically refund a payment already processed.

Maintenance and other ongoing services

For separately purchased maintenance, hosting, SEO, or other recurring services, email us before your next renewal to request cancellation. Any agreed notice period, service end date, or prepaid refund terms are those disclosed in your service agreement. We will confirm the final billing and access dates. Charges from providers you pay directly must be managed with those providers.

Refund requests

Send refund requests to the email above with your project name and payment details. We will provide a written explanation based on your accepted agreement, work completed, and approved expenses. Any refund owed will be returned through the original payment method where possible; processing time depends on the payment provider. Completed work and approved expenses are assessed under the agreed terms rather than a blanket no-refund rule.

Files, domains, and access

On cancellation, we will explain what completed files, credentials, and access can be handed over under your agreement and any outstanding payment requirements. Your own content remains yours. Third-party licenses, subscriptions, and hosting may have separate transfer rules. If hosting or support is ending, arrange backups and a transfer before the confirmed end date.

Consultations and cancellations by BAM’s

Our initial consultation and quote are free. If you cannot attend a booked call, please let us know so we can reschedule. This policy adds no fee for canceling a free consultation. If BAM’s cannot complete an agreed service, we will contact you to arrange handover and return payments for work we will not provide, subject to any more favorable rights or agreed terms.

Which terms apply

Your accepted written project or service agreement sets the specific commercial terms. This policy does not retroactively change an existing agreement, authorize a new cancellation fee, or limit rights provided by applicable law. Changes will be dated on this page. Please ask any questions before approving work or making a payment.
