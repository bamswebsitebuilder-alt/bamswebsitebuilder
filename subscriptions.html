BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

Know your payments, support, and cancellation options.

These installments pay for your website project; they are separate from a maintenance subscription. Your quote and Service Terms explain the full details.

What do I pay upfront?

Your first monthly payment is due according to your accepted quote. The standard plan adds no separate 50% deposit; extras and outside costs are confirmed before you pay.

What support is included?

Reproducible defects in the delivered work are corrected at no charge for 14 days after launch. Optional maintenance is $50/month for up to 60 minutes of minor updates, with an initial response within 2 business days.

Are hosting and a domain included?

Domain registration, hosting, business email, and paid tools are quoted separately unless expressly included. Your quote identifies their costs and renewal responsibilities.

How do I cancel?

Email your quote number to request cancellation. Completed, approved work and authorized outside costs are accounted for, with payments credited. Installments for unperformed future work are not automatically owed.
