Accessibility at BAM’s Website Builder

We aim to make our website usable by people with a wide range of abilities, devices, and assistive technologies. Our ongoing improvements focus on keyboard access, understandable navigation, readable text, responsive layouts, and descriptive controls.

Accessibility is ongoing

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) as a guide for improvements, but have not completed an independent accessibility certification. Some embedded third-party services and older template demonstrations may not offer the same level of access as our core pages.

Need assistance or found a barrier?

Please describe the page, issue, and a way to reach you. We can help you access information or request services by an alternative method.
