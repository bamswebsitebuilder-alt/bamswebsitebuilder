Ownership, renewals, and leaving.

Who owns my domain and website?

Your existing content and client-owned domain remain yours. Domain and hosting accounts should be in your name, with BAM given the access needed to manage the work. Your accepted agreement identifies any BAM-managed account, ownership of custom work, third-party licenses, and the files and access included in handover.

Does hosting include website maintenance?

Only when your written quote includes it. Website build installments, hosting, domain renewals, and maintenance are separate services. There is no automatic upgrade or extra charge when you request support.

How do I cancel or move to another host?

Contact BAM through portal Support or email with your agreement number. Ask for the confirmed service end date, final billing, export or transfer steps, and any agreed handover charges. Arrange the move before hosting ends to reduce disruption. Manage recurring payments with your payment provider as well; a portal status change does not cancel them.

Existing accepted terms continue to apply. See the Service Terms and Cancellation & Refund Policy. This page does not change an existing agreement.

Are backups and uptime guaranteed?

Only commitments stated in your accepted quote apply. Ask which files and databases are backed up, how often, who can restore them, and what the provider covers. BAM does not promise uninterrupted availability or a particular response time through this page.
