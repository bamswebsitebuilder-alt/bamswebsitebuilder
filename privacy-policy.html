Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

BAM's Website Builder respects your privacy. This page explains the information our website may collect, why it may be used, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

When you contact us, request a quote, book a call, create an account, or use client features, you may provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, business information, project details, or files you choose to submit.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use essential browser storage to remember settings and privacy choices. Optional technologies may be used for features such as live chat, analytics, or marketing only according to the preferences you select in the cookie settings panel.

How information may be used

  • To respond to inquiries and provide website services.
  • To operate account, client portal, booking, and support features.
  • To improve website performance and user experience.
  • To understand website usage when analytics permission is enabled.
  • To measure or improve marketing when marketing permission is enabled.

Third-party services

Some features may rely on third-party providers, such as live chat, booking, payment, hosting, or account services. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy terms when you use those features.

Your choices

You can accept all optional technologies, decline them, or choose individual categories. You can reopen the privacy settings at any time using the button below.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to bamswebsitebuilder@gmail.com.
