Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about packages, payments, timelines, ownership, and support.

Service TermsCancellation & Refund PolicyFAQProject Checklist
Which package should I choose?

Starter covers up to 3 pages, Business up to 6, and Premium up to 10. Choose based on both page count and required features. If you need custom functionality, request a free quote before choosing a plan.

How soon can my website launch?

We confirm a project-specific schedule after reviewing the scope and your materials. Tell us your target launch date when you inquire. Content delivery, feedback, integrations, and extra requirements can affect the timeline.

How many revisions are included?

Your written quote specifies the revision rounds and review period. Ask for those details before paying. Requests beyond the agreed scope can require a separate quote.

Do I own my website?

Your content remains yours. The agreement should explain ownership or licensing of the finished website, delivery of source files and credentials, and any final payment requirements. Confirm those details before work begins.

Are hosting, a domain, and business email included?

Confirm each item in your quote. Domain connection is a separate listed service; it does not include domain purchase or renewal unless expressly agreed. Hosting, email subscriptions, and other provider fees should be clearly identified before payment.

Do you write the text and provide photos?

Tell us whether you will provide content or need help creating it. Content writing, image sourcing, and licensing must be included in the agreed scope and price; do not assume they are included in every package.

How do the monthly payments compare with one-time prices?

One-time packages start at $250, $500, and $850. The displayed monthly rates over 12 payments total $249.96, $500.04, and $849.96. These small differences come from the displayed monthly amounts. Custom extras and any applicable taxes should be confirmed before checkout.

What happens after the 12 payments?

The advertised payment plan covers 12 payments toward your website project. Final handover and any remaining account responsibilities follow your written agreement. Ongoing maintenance, hosting, or other services are separate unless expressly included; confirm what continues and what ends before purchase.

Can I cancel my project or automatic payments?

Yes, you can request cancellation by emailing BAM’s and manage automatic payments in your PayPal account. Any balance or refund depends on the agreed terms and work completed. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Can you update or redesign an existing website?

Yes. Request a quote with your current website address and the changes you need. Maintenance starts at $50 per month and redesign work starts at $200; the final scope and price are confirmed for your project.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or sales?

Search setup can include titles, descriptions, headings, and other agreed improvements. We do not promise a particular ranking, sales total, or number of leads. Results depend on competition, content, ongoing work, and other factors.

What should I send to get started?

Use the Project Preparation Checklist for your business information, pages, branding, content, and access needs. You can send an inquiry even if some materials are not ready.

Ready to discuss your project?

Your initial consultation and quote are free.
