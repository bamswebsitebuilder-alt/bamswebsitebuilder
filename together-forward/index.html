FAQ

A few things you might wonder.

Can this template work for our cause?

Yes. The colors, logo, programs, photographs, and calls to action can be adapted to your organization and the community you serve.

Can we accept recurring donations?

The design includes a monthly giving option. Live recurring payments require your organization’s own donation provider and account.

Where do our reports and nonprofit details go?

The impact section can link to your approved annual reports, financial summaries, and verified registration details. This demo does not claim nonprofit or tax-exempt status.
