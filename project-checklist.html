Project Preparation Checklist

Bring what you have. Use this list to organize your project and tell us where you need help.

Service TermsCancellation & Refund PolicyFAQProject Checklist

Business details

  • Business name, contact information, service area, hours, and social links.
  • The main action visitors should take: call, book, buy, or request a quote.

Pages and features

  • A page list, such as Home, About, Services, Gallery, and Contact.
  • Forms, booking, store, payment, or client account features you need.
  • Your preferred package, budget, and target launch date.

Branding and examples

  • Your logo in the best quality available, plus preferred colors and fonts.
  • Two or three websites you like and what you like about each.
  • Tell us which items you need help creating.

Text and images

  • Draft page text, service descriptions, pricing, and product information.
  • Photos, video, testimonials, and permission to publish them.
  • Tell us if writing, photography, or image sourcing is needed so it can be quoted.

Accounts and access

  • Your current website address and the providers used for your domain, hosting, email, and booking.
  • Invite BAM’s through a provider’s collaborator or delegated-access feature when available.
  • Do not put passwords, payment card details, or private customer records in the public inquiry form.

Before you approve the project

  • Review the written scope, total price, payment schedule, revision rounds, and launch timeline.
  • Confirm hosting, domain renewals, ongoing services, ownership, and source-file handover.
  • Review the service terms and cancellation policy and ask about anything unclear.

Before launch

  • Check names, phone numbers, email addresses, hours, pricing, and page text.
  • Review the mobile layout and test the contact, booking, and checkout flows that apply.
  • Confirm who receives inquiries and who handles future updates.
