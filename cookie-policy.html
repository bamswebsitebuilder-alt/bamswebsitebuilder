Cookie Policy

Last reviewed: November 9, 2026. This page explains the cookie choices available on this website. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

What cookies do

Cookies and similar browser storage can support security and basic functionality, remember preferences, and help us understand visits. Some third-party services may also use storage when you interact with embedded content.

Categories and your choices

  • Necessary: Supports core site functions and records your privacy choice. These cannot be disabled through our consent controls.
  • Functional: Optional features such as live chat.
  • Analytics: Optional website-use measurement. Google Analytics is configured to load after analytics consent on public pages.
  • Marketing: Optional advertising and campaign measurement, where enabled.

You may accept, reject, or choose categories in the cookie preference panel. You can also clear browser storage or use your browser settings. Global Privacy Control signals are respected by our analytics script.

Third-party content

Our site may link to or display services operated by providers such as Google, payment providers, scheduling tools, and review widgets. Those services have their own privacy practices. Some embedded content may load separately from this site's consent system; please review the provider notices when interacting with it.
