Service Terms

Know what is included, how your project is approved, and what to expect before you pay.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Your written project agreement

Before approving work or paying, review your written quote or project agreement. It should identify your package, page count, features, deliverables, total price, payment schedule, revisions, timeline, ongoing costs, and ownership or handover terms. Ask BAM’s to confirm anything that is missing. This page explains the process; it is not a substitute for those project-specific terms.

Scope and changes

The agreed scope describes the pages and functions we will build. Extra pages, features, content writing, or integrations outside that scope require a written description and price that you approve before the extra work begins. A portfolio demo illustrates a design or feature; its entire functionality is not automatically included in every package.

Prices and payments

One-time packages currently start at $250 for Starter, $500 for Business, and $850 for Premium. Custom requirements may change your quote. Website payment plans are advertised as 12 monthly payments: $20.83, $41.67, or $70.83 per month. At those rates, the scheduled totals are $249.96, $500.04, and $849.96 respectively. Confirm the selected plan, final project price, any approved extras, applicable taxes, and payment schedule before paying.

Schedule and client materials

The delivery schedule is confirmed for your project after its scope and required materials are reviewed. Provide accurate business information, text, images, and feedback, and flag any deadline before work starts. Missing materials, delayed approvals, or approved scope changes may affect the schedule; revised dates should be confirmed in writing.

Revisions and approval

The number of included revision rounds and the review period are set in your written quote. Review the design, page content, links, forms, and business details before approving launch. Changes outside the agreed scope or after launch should be quoted separately unless included in your agreement.

Hosting, domains, and third-party costs

Confirm whether hosting, domain registration or renewal, business email, paid software, licensed assets, and third-party services are included or billed separately. Domain connection is listed as a separate service and is not the same as buying or renewing a domain. Ongoing provider fees and account responsibilities should be identified in your quote before purchase.

Ownership and handover

Your own content remains yours. Ownership or licensing of the finished website, source files, and design, along with any payment requirements for transfer, must be specified in your agreement. Third-party software and assets remain subject to their licenses. Ask for a written list of files, accounts, and access included in your handover.

Support after launch

Website maintenance currently starts at $50 per month as a separate service. Its update allowance, response expectations, billing period, and cancellation terms should be confirmed in writing. The 12-payment website plan does not by itself include an ongoing maintenance agreement. Any hosting or support after the plan ends must be separately agreed.

Content and permissions

Provide content you own or have permission to use, including logos, photos, text, testimonials, and customer information. Tell us about any special privacy, accessibility, industry, or business requirements so they can be included in the scope. Basic search setup does not promise a particular search ranking or number of leads.

Cancellation, refunds, and existing agreements

See our Cancellation & Refund Policy for request instructions and how completed work, payments, and handover are reviewed. Your accepted agreement determines the specific commercial terms. These service terms add no new cancellation penalty, do not retroactively change an existing agreement, and do not limit rights provided by applicable law.
