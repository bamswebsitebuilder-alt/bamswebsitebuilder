WEBSITE TIPS

How to compare website quotes

Compare the scope and full cost, not only the headline price.

← All website tips

Compare what is included

Count the pages, forms, language versions, and integrations in each quote. Ask who supplies the text and images. A lower price may cover fewer deliverables, so compare the same scope.

Check the total cost

Look at upfront payments, installment totals, and separately billed items. Confirm domain renewals, hosting, business email, licenses, and maintenance. Ask which costs repeat and who is responsible for paying them.

Understand review and handover

Confirm the delivery estimate, what starts the schedule, and how many revision rounds are included. Ask what files and account access you receive, and when ownership of the custom work transfers.

Know your next step

Review support and cancellation terms before agreeing or paying. With BAM, an inquiry comes first, followed by a written quote and acceptance before a payment link. Use the current Prices and Website Plans pages to explore your options.
