The Organization

OUR MISSION

HYPE 7V7 develops football players and young people on and off the field through coaching, education, discipline, leadership and recruiting guidance.

THE SKILLZ CENTER

The Tampa-based training facility provides indoor training space, performance equipment and a multi-sport community.

FOUNDER

Lawrance Toafili, a St. Petersburg native and former Florida State University running back, founded HYPE 7V7 to support the next generation of athletes.

OUR STANDARDS

Accountability, development, respect and family involvement guide the program.
