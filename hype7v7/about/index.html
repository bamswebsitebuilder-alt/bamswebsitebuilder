About us

MORE THAN FOOTBALL

Hype 7v7 is committed to developing complete football players and dependable young people who are prepared for success both on and off the field. Founded and led by Lawrance Toafili, Hype 7v7 combines competitive 7v7 football with structured coaching, academic accountability, life skills, and recruiting guidance.

Our mission goes beyond football. We emphasize education, discipline, leadership, character, and responsibility, helping athletes stay accountable in the classroom while preparing for the challenges of adulthood. We work to guide players through the recruiting process, connect them with opportunities to continue their athletic careers, and prepare them for the next level, whether that means college football or success beyond the game.

At Hype 7v7, the goal is not just to build better athletes, but to help young people become better students, leaders, teammates, and individuals. We want every athlete to leave our program more prepared, more confident, and better equipped for whatever comes next.

Lawrance ToafiliFounder & Program Leader
Founder

LAWRANCE TOAFILI

Lawrance Toafili brings firsthand experience at the highest levels of football to Hype 7v7. A St. Petersburg native and former Pinellas Park High School standout, Toafili went on to play running back at Florida State University, where he competed against top college talent and earned MVP honors in the 2023 ACC Championship Game. His journey through high school recruiting and major college football gives him a personal understanding of the work, discipline, and commitment it takes to reach the next level.

Through Hype 7v7, Toafili is building more than a competitive football program. His vision is to provide Tampa-area athletes with a structured environment where effort, accountability, respect, and personal growth are expected every day. From 14U through 18U, the goal is to develop athletes at every stage while helping them understand the responsibilities that come with being a student, teammate, and young leader.

Under his leadership, Hype 7v7 emphasizes education, academic accountability, character development, and recruiting guidance. The mission is to help athletes recognize opportunities, prepare for college, and develop the habits needed to succeed beyond football. By combining high-level competition with mentorship and life lessons, Toafili aims to help every athlete leave the program better prepared for the next chapter of their journey.

Our standards

WHAT WE STAND ON

01

Accountability

We hold our athletes accountable on and off the field, teaching them responsibility for effort, grades, and actions.

02

Development First

Consistent coaching, meaningful reps, confidence and discipline on and off the field.

03

Respect

Respect teammates, opponents, officials and the community.

04

Family

Families stay involved through volunteering and GroupMe updates, building a stronger athlete community.

Expectations

HOW WE OPERATE

Athlete expectations

  • Travel together when arranged by the program
  • Grades monitored throughout the season
  • Attend at least 80% of practices
  • Compete with confidence while respecting officials

Parent & family expectations

  • Program coordinates travel, lodging and meals for distant events
  • Arrive at the assigned meeting point for local events
  • Support athletes and allow coaches to coach
  • Complete medical and eligibility paperwork
  • Raise concerns directly with coaches
  • Stay involved and volunteer

Coach expectations

  • Communicate practice plans and event details
  • Prioritize development while competing to win
  • Starting positions and reps are earned
  • Provide film feedback
  • Advocate for college opportunities

THE SKILLZ CENTER

Hype 7v7 trains at The Skillz Center, a Tampa-based non-profit sports training facility that also runs basketball programs for local youth. The partnership gives athletes year-round indoor training space, performance equipment and a community of multi-sport athletes.
