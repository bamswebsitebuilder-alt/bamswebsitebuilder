PAYMENT OPTIONS

Understand the costs.
Make informed decisions.

Scottie Bonding Agency accepts cash, Zelle, and Cash App. Call (727) 323-1110 to discuss costs and payment arrangements.

01

Fees & written terms

Request a written explanation of the costs, any additional charges, and applicable terms.

02

Accepted payment methods

Cash, Zelle, and Cash App are accepted. Contact Scottie Bonding Agency to arrange payment; call the agency for payment instructions.

03

Discuss payment arrangements

Ask whether any plan is offered, who qualifies, and which written terms apply. Availability is not guaranteed.

Before making a payment

  • Confirm the provider and the purpose of the payment.
  • Read the agreement and understand all obligations.
  • Ask about receipts, cancellation terms, and any collateral.
  • Use only a verified payment channel.

Arrange payments directly with the agency. Call to confirm payment instructions before sending funds.
