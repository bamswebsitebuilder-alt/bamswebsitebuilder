BEFORE YOU AGREE

Ask for a clear explanation.

A helpful conversation should make the terms and your obligations easier to understand.

  • Request a written explanation of the costs.
  • Confirm the provider’s authorization and service area.
  • Understand any signer or collateral responsibilities.
  • Ask how to keep track of required dates and next steps.
