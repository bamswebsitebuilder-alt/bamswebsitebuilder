Meet Braion Moreland

Founder & Web Designer

I started BAM's Website Builder to create something meaningful—not only for myself, but for my son, my future children, and the generations that follow them.

My interest in technology began when I was young. Watching the technology-driven storylines on NCIS sparked my curiosity about computers and what could be created through code. I began learning coding in middle school and later attended trade school to continue pursuing something I genuinely loved.

Today, I use that passion to help small businesses build professional, responsive websites that strengthen their presence, communicate their services clearly, and support their growth.
