Studio policies

What to know before your visit.

This additional website page keeps booking expectations clear.

Deposits

A nonrefundable deposit may be required to secure an appointment and will be applied to the final balance.

Cancellations

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice. Late cancellations may require a new deposit.

Late arrivals

Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may need to be shortened or rescheduled.

Guests

To maintain a calm studio environment, please arrive alone unless approved beforehand.

Repairs

Contact the studio within 48 hours if you experience an issue. Repairs after that period may have a fee.

Payments

Accepted payment methods can be customized before launch.
