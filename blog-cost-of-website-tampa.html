How much does a small business website cost in Tampa Bay?

The cost of a website depends on what you need it to do, how many pages it has, whether you provide the content, and which features need custom setup. A simple informational site and a booking or client-portal website are different projects.

What affects the quote?

Pages and content. More unique pages, writing and photography generally require more work. Features. Booking, forms, online payments and integrations may add complexity. Design. Custom branding and interactive elements can increase the scope. Ongoing costs. Domains, hosting, email services and maintenance may be separate from the initial build.

Questions to ask before paying

Who owns the domain? Are revisions included? Is mobile design included? What happens after launch? Are hosting, email and updates recurring charges? What will you need to provide?
